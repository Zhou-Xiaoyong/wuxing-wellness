Sunday morning, and the hair in the shower drain is a small grey mouse of it. You have not changed shampoo. Nothing hurts. The bloodwork from last spring says every line is normal.

The word is doing two jobs here. In Chinese medicine the kidney (肾, shèn) is a system with a season, an emotion and a store of reserve. It is not the pair of organs filtering your blood, and if those are the worry, a creatinine and an eGFR say more than a page can.

A store is the one thing you cannot inspect. You can feel a stuck shoulder, not a low reserve. So the tradition points at two tissues instead, and both are slow on purpose. This is not medical advice. Thinning hair and aching bone each have a dozen causes, and half need tests rather than tea.

## The kidney's report comes from two places you can see

One chapter of the Inner Canon (《黄帝内经》) does in a dozen characters what most writing on this subject spends a book on: the kidney is the root of sealing, where essence is kept, its blossom is in the hair, what it fills is the bone (肾者，主蛰，封藏之本，精之处也；其华在发，其充在骨), from the Plain Questions (《素问·六节藏象论》).

Twelve characters for the container. Eight for the only two windows into it.

Blossom (华, huá) is the flowering, the part that shows above ground. Fill (充, chōng) is depth, what a structure is packed with. Surface and substrate. Neither is a lab value, which looks deliberate: the canon never offers a way to measure essence, only a way to watch it.

## Hair is a receipt dated three months ago

A scalp hair spends two to seven years growing, about two weeks in transition, then some three months resting before it lets go. The shaft travels half an inch a month. Put those together and you get the most useful fact about shedding. Today's loss was decided last season.

That delay is why the shed arrives with no cause attached. A fever, a surgery, a birth, a diet that dropped ten pounds in six weeks: any one can push a large share of follicles into rest at once, and each reports two to three months later, the reason long since filed away. Clinicians call it telogen effluvium, and the lag is half the diagnosis.

Fifty to a hundred strands a day is ordinary. What matters is your own baseline, which almost nobody keeps. Count what comes out at the next wash, then again four weeks on. Same shampoo, same towel.

One blood figure is worth holding in your hand. Ferritin prints as normal from around 15 ng/mL, while the hair-loss literature treats 30, sometimes 40, as the level a follicle wants. Ask for the number, not the word. Protein matters more bluntly: the shaft is almost pure keratin, and a very low-protein diet moves follicles into rest as a group.

The tradition gives hair a second owner: hair is the surplus of blood (发为血之余). That is the liver's department, which is why the plate advice on the [liver page](/blog/how-to-strengthen-liver-naturally-chinese-medicine/) and the plate advice here arrive at the same dinner.

## Bone is a bank that stops taking deposits at thirty

Bone is the other window, on a longer clock. You build peak mass by about thirty, then begin a slow withdrawal. For women the first five to seven years after menopause take a steep slice. The canon pairs teeth with the same logic, calling them the surplus of bone (齿为骨之余).

Bone is not calcium with a shape. Mineral is roughly two thirds of its weight; most of the rest is collagen, the lattice the mineral sits in. Which is why a plate of the right number can still build nothing. Bone answers to strain. Stillness reads as an instruction to dismantle. Loading reads as an instruction to spend.

Three ounces of canned sardines with the bones holds about 325 mg of calcium; a cup of cooked collards, 268 mg; a cup of milk, 300. Most adults need 1,000 a day; women past fifty or men past seventy, 1,200.

Vitamin D fails quietly in November. Above roughly 40 degrees north, skin makes almost none from sunlight between November and February, however clear the day. The standing intake is 600 IU, or 800 after seventy.

One folk rule is worth retiring. Bone broth is not a calcium supplement. Collagen does not arrive in your blood as collagen, and the mineral a four-hour pot draws out is a fraction of a cup of milk. The tradition's bone broth (骨汤, gǔ tāng) earns its place as warmth, salt and gelatin on a cold night, not as a bone-density strategy.

## Ninety days is the unit, and that is the good news

Set the two clocks side by side. A hair cycle runs about a season. A bone remodeling unit spends two to four weeks tearing down and four to six months rebuilding. Nothing here reports back in a fortnight.

Which explains a verdict most people reach. Three weeks on a new powder, nothing changed, abandoned. No information was gathered. The trial had not finished.

Winter is Water's season, and the old instruction to tonify then, with the result at the thaw, lands on the same calendar. Treat in the cold months. Read the report in spring.

Pick a date and measure three things: strands at the next wash, the ferritin figure, and your height against a doorframe once a year. Height is the only bone number available at home, and an inch and a half earns a conversation.

## The pantry answer, and its honest limit

Black sesame (黑芝麻, hēi zhī ma) is the food the tradition reaches for first, and a fourth-century alchemist is why. Ge Hong's manual (《抱朴子》) promises that a hundred days of it clears every chronic complaint, that two years turn white hair black and three regrow lost teeth. Read it as an advertisement for a crop, and it still says what the food was for.

The nutrient table half-agrees. An ounce of whole black sesame carries about 280 mg of calcium, mostly in the hull, which is why hulled white sesame is a far weaker source. Sesame also brings oxalate, and oxalate holds much of that calcium back. Grinding helps you absorb the seed. It does not change the chemistry.

If calcium is the goal, fish are the better bet: a skeleton is already the right shape. An ounce of the dried baby shrimp (虾皮, xiā pí) Chinese cooking scatters into soup holds a similar 280 mg with far more sodium, so rinse them and salt the pot last.

The limits deserve stating. Hair that sheds with scaling, scarring or a smooth bald patch is a dermatologist's question. A bone that breaks from standing height is a fracture, not a deficiency. Sesame has been a labeled allergen on US packaging since 2023, so a first spoon deserves to be treated as one.

Start with the date, not the powder. Circle a day ninety days out, write the strand count and the ferritin figure beside it, and let a season do what a week cannot. If the second circle shows nothing, ask for a thyroid panel.

## Quick self-check

| Statement | Feels like me |
| --- | --- |
| My hair comes out in handfuls at the wash, and it began a season after something else happened | ☐ |
| The hair at my part is thinner than it was two years ago | ☐ |
| A blood test called my ferritin normal and I never saw the number | ☐ |
| A chair seat feels higher than it used to, or my height has dropped | ☐ |
| Between November and March I get almost no direct sun | ☐ |
| I am past fifty and have never had a conversation about bone density | ☐ |

Four or more, and the slow tissues deserve a look before anything else here. Two or fewer, and the hair in the drain is probably ordinary turnover. Rows three and six are the pair to watch: a figure you were handed and never read, and a conversation most people over fifty have still not had.
